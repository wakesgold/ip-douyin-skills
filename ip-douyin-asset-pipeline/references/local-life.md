# Local-Life / Restaurant / Store-Owner Enhancement

Use this when the account is for restaurants, local services, offline stores, city businesses, or store-founder IP.

## Core Judgment

```text
起号成功概率 = 创始人IP势能 × 产品视觉冲击力 × 本地流量匹配精度 × 配合投入稳定性
```

## Eight Interview Dimensions

| Dimension | Required Questions | Assets To Extract | Low-Signal Warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 品类动机 | Why this category? What did others fail to do? What cost did you pay? | category insight, origin story | only says it can make money |
| 手艺与产品壁垒 | What is different? Which ingredient/process is non-negotiable? What looks wow on camera? | visual selling point, craft detail | difference only explained by low price |
| 个人故事 | What before opening? Most stubborn/crazy choice? Why this city? | contrast persona, local affection | no turn, no cost, no quote |
| 本地基因 | Which local KOLs/peers/users matter? How do locals choose? | cold-start resources, trust chain | no local sense |
| 经营现状 | revenue, ticket size, turnover, busy/quiet time, repeat rate, pain | KPI, schedule, testimony | cannot explain data |
| 竞品认知 | Who is doing well locally? What are others wrong about? | industry view, anti-patterns | only insults peers |
| 投入预期 | time, money,出镜, no-result tolerance, capacity if viral | cooperation level, risk boundary | wants fast result without input |
| 审美与表达 | What accounts do they watch? What photos/videos exist? Premium or烟火气? | style direction, visual baseline | taste conflicts with positioning |

## Session Deliverables

After a 60-90 minute session, produce:

1. **Founder IP positioning card**: who I am, why trust me, what makes me different.
2. **Content ammunition library**: at least 10 story/product/view assets.
3. **First content columns**: 3-5 repeatable series.
4. **Launch risk judgment**: product, cooperation, delivery capacity, budget, or expectation shortboard.

## Reusable Columns

- 创始人故事：“我为什么非要做这个业务？”
- 行业避坑：“别再被 XX 忽悠了”
- 普通人逆袭：“我从 XX 到 XX，靠的不是运气”
- 方法论拆解：“照着这 3 步，你也能……”
- 案例复盘：“这个客户为什么能从 A 到 B？”
- 认知反差：“真正厉害的人，从不……”
- 幕后日常：“今天见了一个客户，我更确定一件事”
